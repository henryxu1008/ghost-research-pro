# Ghost Research Pro — MVP PRD (Draft)

## Product hypothesis
A research-focused Ghost theme can help independent analysts, investment research publishers and B2B intelligence teams present reports and convert readers into paid members. This is unvalidated until customer discovery.

## Target users
- Independent professional research publishers using Ghost.
- B2B intelligence newsletters and specialist industry media.

## P0 user journeys
1. Anonymous visitor discovers research from home, tag archive or author page.
2. Visitor opens a report with readable headings, tables, citations and responsive layout.
3. Visitor sees a native Ghost member or paid-member CTA and signs up using Ghost Portal.
4. Existing paid member views content permitted by Ghost visibility settings.
5. Publisher configures branding and navigation using Ghost-supported settings.

## P0 templates
Home, post, tag/archive, author, membership/pricing landing page, 404; mobile responsive states.

## Acceptance criteria
- All P0 pages render on a supported Ghost version without theme validation errors.
- Anonymous, free and paid member states respect Ghost's native access controls.
- Keyboard navigation, semantic headings, focus visibility and mobile layouts are verified.
- A fresh Ghost installation can install the packaged theme and reproduce demo flows from documentation.
- No credentials, private customer content or proprietary assets are committed.

## Out of scope
Custom payment processing, financial market feeds, advanced search service, automatic PDF production, analytics backend, custom user permissions.

## Open decisions
Exact Ghost version compatibility; theme license; price positioning; customer willingness to pay; search UX; demo hosting.
