# Market validation — research plan

**Status:** Research protocol only; customer interviews and market demand have NOT yet been validated.

## Evidence collection
For each of at least 10 competing Ghost themes record: name, URL, snapshot date, price, license, target audience, report archive, membership CTA, navigation, accessibility, update recency, strengths and gaps.

For at least 20 commercial Ghost publications record: URL, industry, observable business model, existing theme/customization signals, and contact route where public. Do not infer revenue from website appearance.

## Customer discovery
Interview at least five prospective publishers about their current Ghost setup, paid publishing workflows, theme limitations, procurement budget and willingness to buy. Ask for examples of actual workarounds and purchases rather than hypothetical enthusiasm.

## Go / pivot gate
Proceed to full implementation only after documenting at least three recurring unmet needs and two credible purchase-intent signals; otherwise adjust the segment or offer paid customization first.

## Candidate hypotheses to test
- A report library with strong taxonomy is more valuable than a generic blog archive.
- Trust-focused analyst profiles and report metadata help research businesses.
- Membership conversion UX can be improved without replacing Ghost Portal.
